# FiKR&CD — Forum for Indus-Kohistani Research & Cultural Development

**Community-led language research, documentation, preservation and digital language technology for Indus-Kohistani and related Dardic languages.**

**Co-founded by Saif Ullah Jailani and Dr Hussain Ahmad Faizy**  
**Project leadership:** Saif Ullah Jailani — Project Director / Co-founder  
**Region:** Kohistan & Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan

FiKR&CD connects community-led linguistic research with practical digital preservation, written language resources, language technology and multilingual publishing. Its work includes Indus-Kohistani language documentation, dialect research, orthography and typography, datasets, digital archives and preservation-oriented software.

## Saif Ullah and FiKR&CD

Saif Ullah Jailani is a multilingual translator, localization specialist and language-technology practitioner whose work connects **Arabic ↔ Urdu translation, localization, LQA, MTPE and AI language data** with **Indus-Kohistani and Shina language documentation and digital preservation**.
